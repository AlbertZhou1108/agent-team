# Community Reading Club Event Plan

> Fictional demo scenario containing no real participant or customer data.

## 1. Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Format | Casual exchange · Bring-your-own-book sharing |
| Headcount | 15 adults (including staff) |
| Time | Saturday 14:00–15:30 (90 minutes) |
| Venue | Community reading room (free) |
| Budget Cap | 300 RMB |
| Books & Catering | Attendees bring their own books; no book purchases or catering |

## 2. 90-Minute Schedule

| Time | Segment | Plan | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 14:00–14:10 | Icebreaker | Host intro and ground rules: anyone can speak, pass, or disagree respectfully | Host |
| 14:10–14:40 | Open Sharing | ~90 seconds per person with buffer; attendees without a book can share recent reading or answer a prompt card | Host (timekeeper) |
| 14:40–15:05 | Roundtable | Free discussion on shared topics; encourage newcomers to speak | Facilitators |
| 15:05–15:20 | Next Pick | Agree on one book or theme for the next meetup | Facilitators |
| 15:20–15:30 | Wrap-up | Collect feedback, optional group photo, announce next date | Host |

## 3. Staffing

> Total cap is 15 attendees, consisting of 4 staff below plus 11 general attendees.

| Role | Count | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Host | 1 | Opening, timekeeping, transitions, closing |
| Facilitators | 2 | Guide discussion, welcome newcomers, balance airtime |
| Note-taker / Timer | 1 | Capture highlights/next topics and signal time cues |
| General Attendees | 11 | Share and discuss; newcomers get priority turns |

## 4. Budget (300 RMB Cap)

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Name stickers & sign-in sheet printing | 40 RMB |
| Discussion prompt cards & sticky notes | 50 RMB |
| Contingency reserve | 150 RMB |
| **Total** | **240 RMB** |

> Free venue, no book purchases, and no catering (bottled water removed). Line items may adjust as long as the total stays within 300 RMB.

## 5. Host Tips

- Set a relaxed tone upfront: no judgment, listening silently is welcome, invite newcomers warmly.
- Keep sharing around 90 seconds using gentle cues rather than abrupt cutoffs.
- Proactively invite newcomers to ask questions or share so regulars do not dominate.
- Keep the roundtable exploratory rather than debating "right vs. wrong" answers.

## 6. Contingencies

- **Overcapacity**: Use a waitlist above 15 people; waitlisted guests join only if someone cancels or in the next session.
- **Slow conversation**: Facilitators draw starter questions from the prompt cards.
- **Venue conflict**: Reconfirm the room reservation ahead of time; fall back to an online text sharing relay in the group if bumped.
- **Early departures**: Keep the schedule moving and capture key takeaways in the group afterwards.
- **Weather / disruptions**: Host posts a notice 1 day prior and announces a 1-week postponement if needed.
